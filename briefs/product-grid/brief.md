# Product listing

Build the product listing for a category page of Hearthcove, an online
kitchenware shop. It is a full-width section of the category page, below
the site header.

Start with the category heading and the results summary. Then show the
products as cards. Each card shows the product image, its name, its price
in the given currency, the original price struck through when the product
is on sale, the star rating with the number of reviews, the product's
tags, and an add-to-cart button with the given label.

The component receives the props described by the `Props` interface.
Images are served from the given paths. It only needs to render; the
buttons do not need to do anything.
