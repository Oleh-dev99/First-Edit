# Customer testimonials

Build the testimonials section for the marketing site of Kiteward, an
expense management app for finance teams. It is a full-width section of
the home page.

Start with the section heading and the subheading under it. Then show
the testimonials, each as a card with the customer's quote, a rating of
one to five stars, and the author: their name, job title and company.
The quotes are the heart of the section, so make them easy to read, and
give every card the same treatment so the section looks calm and even.

The component receives the props described by the `Props` interface. It
only needs to render.
