# Pricing table

Build the pricing section of the marketing site for Taskflow, a project
management tool. It is a full-width page section that sits between other
sections of the landing page.

Open with the section heading and subheading. Then present the plans so
visitors can compare them at a glance. For each plan, show its name, its
monthly price in the given currency, the one-line description, the
included features as a checklist, and a call-to-action button with the
plan's label. A plan can carry a short badge label such as "Most popular";
show it on that plan. One plan is highlighted: make it stand out as the
recommended choice.

The component receives `Props` from props.ts. It only needs to render; the
buttons do not need to do anything.
