# Pricing table

Build the pricing section of the marketing site for Tasklume, a project
management tool. It is a full-width page section that sits between other
sections of the landing page.

Open with the section heading and subheading. Then present the plans so
visitors can compare them at a glance. For each plan, show its name, its
monthly price in the given currency, its description, the included
features as a checklist, and a call-to-action button with the plan's
label. A plan can carry a badge label such as "Most popular"; show it on
that plan. One plan is highlighted: make it stand out as the recommended
choice.

The component receives the props described by the `Props` interface. It
only needs to render; the buttons do not need to do anything.
