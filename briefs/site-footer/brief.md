# Website footer

Build the footer of the marketing website of Wickerlight, scheduling
software for clinics and studios. It is a full-width section at the
bottom of every page.

Show the company name and its tagline. Show the link columns in the
given order, each with its title and its links. Add the newsletter
sign-up: the prompt, then an email field with its label and placeholder,
and a button with the given label. End with the legal line at the very
bottom of the footer.

The component receives the props described by the `Props` interface. It
only needs to render; the links and the sign-up do not need to work.
