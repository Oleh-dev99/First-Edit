Layout breakage: blind labelling

Each screen shows two pictures of the same component: on the left before a change, on the right after a change to its content or its screen. The line above the pictures names the component and the change. Answer one question for each pair:

Does the right-hand picture show layout breakage that the left-hand one does not?

1 broken
content is cut off, spills out of its box, overlaps other content, is pushed off the screen, or the page scrolls sideways
2 fine
none of that; awkward wrapping and words broken across lines are fine
3 other
something else looks wrong (a squeezed column, missing content, a collapsed element), but not the kinds above
4 unsure
cannot tell

Only what is new on the right counts: a flaw that the left-hand picture already shows does not. When a picture is wider than its screen, a dashed red line marks the screen's edge; whatever lies past it can only be seen by scrolling the page sideways. Judge each pair on its own.

Keys: 1–4 answer and move on · N write a note (optional; Enter keeps it) · Z undo the last answer · ← → previous and next · click a picture to zoom, Esc to close. Every answer is saved at once: close this tab whenever you like, and start the tool again to carry on.
